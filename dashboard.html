Total Orders vs Audits — Pod View

Per pod: FnV orders and FnV IGCC orders summed over the same pre/post audit windows as the IGCC % — pre = days before the 1st audit, post = days after; window length = gap between the pod's audits (7 days for single-audit pods). IGCC Before vs After — Charts

Each chart shows daily IGCC % over time for one pod or city. The red dashed line is the audit date: the blue line to the left of it is IGCC before the audit, to the right is after. If the line drops after the red line, IGCC improved. Use the range buttons to zoom the window around the audit date.

IGCC Reason Bucketing (RCA)

Root-cause buckets — Pod (handling/hygiene at the pod), CX (customer perception / false claim), Sourcing (upstream quality) or WH (warehouse / packing) — measured as IGCC % (bucket claims ÷ FnV orders) before vs after each audit, using the same shared-window rule as the rest of the dashboard. Source: analytics.public.rca_daily_igcc_5 joined with the audit and daily IGCC files.

Pod × audit detail

One row per audit window: bucket claims and IGCC % before vs after the audit. Click headers to sort.
Windows: the days between two audits form ONE shared window used for both "IGCC post" of the earlier audit and "IGCC pre" of the later audit — so post 1 and pre 2 are always the same % (e.g. audits on Sep 1 and Sep 13 → pre 1 = Aug 20–31, the shared Sep 2–12 window gives post 1 AND pre 2, post 2 = Sep 13 → today). Pre of the 1st audit = same number of days before it as the gap to the next audit. 7 days for pods with a single audit or no audit. The "Win days" / "Window days" columns show the days used. QNP = IGCC orders ÷ total FnV orders. Score % = overall pod audit score. FNV% = FnV-section compliance.
